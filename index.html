04 / UNDER THE HOOD

Know the difference.

An attractive number without a reliable probability is just a guess. Here's how we keep the analysis honest.
01

REMOVE THE VIG

Normalize a sportsbook's implied probabilities to remove its built-in margin. Compare multiple other books at the identical market and line.

02

FIND THE BEST PRICE

Compare odds from every available bookmaker. The no-vig reference excludes the sportsbook offering the selected best price.

03

CALCULATE EXPECTED VALUE

EV = probability × net payout − probability of loss. Positive market-proxy EV is a screening signal, not evidence of genuine advantage.

04

VERIFY WITH A MODEL

An independently built and backtested model would be needed to label an edge as statistically supported. That model isn't connected yet.

WHAT THIS SITE DOES — AND DOESN'T DO

Market probabilities and past-game hit rates are not independent forecasts. Prices, injuries, roof status and weather can change. Player history and injury notes are manually entered until a verified ingestion pipeline is connected. No validated player-prop model is connected. Use for research, not guaranteed returns.
